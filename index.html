✦ How Verification Works

Every seller on AREWA SQUARE goes through a strict 5-step human verification process before they can list a single product.

01

Submit Your Application

Fill in your business name, category, location (LGA/ward), WhatsApp number, and a brief description of what you sell. This takes under 3 minutes.

02

Identity Confirmation

Our team contacts you on WhatsApp to confirm your identity. We verify your phone number is real and active, and that you are who you say you are.

03

Business Check

We confirm your business location is real — either through a photo of your shop, market stall, or operating location. Home-based sellers are accepted.

04

Manual Review by AREWA SQUARE Team

Our team reviews everything within 24–48 hours. We check for duplicates, fake businesses, and any red flags before approval.

05

Receive Your Permanent Shop Number

Once approved, you are assigned a unique permanent shop number (e.g. Shop #021). This number belongs to your business forever and cannot be transferred.

⏱️ Timeline: 24–48 hours after submission.  |  💰 Cost: Free to apply.  |  📞 Questions? WhatsApp us

✦ Seller Rules & Standards

To protect buyers and maintain the trust AREWA SQUARE is built on, all sellers must follow these rules at all times.

✅

Sell Only What You Actually Have

Do not list products you cannot supply. Misleading buyers about product availability is grounds for immediate suspension.

💰

Display Honest Prices

The price shown to a buyer must be the price they pay. No hidden charges. No price changes after a buyer contacts you.

📞

Respond to Buyers Promptly

Respond to buyer messages within 24 hours. Sellers with consistently poor response rates risk losing their shop listing.

🛡️

No Fraud or Deception

Any seller found collecting payment without delivering goods will be permanently removed and reported. We take fraud very seriously.

🖼️

Use Real Product Photos

Only upload photos of your actual products. Using stock images or photos from other sellers is not permitted.

🔢

Your Shop Number is Non-Transferable

Your shop number belongs to you and your registered business only. It cannot be sold, lent, or shared with another person or business.

⚠️

Consequences for Violations

Minor violations result in a warning. Serious violations result in immediate shop suspension. Fraud results in permanent removal and blacklisting.

By selling on AREWA SQUARE you agree to these rules. Questions? WhatsApp our team

✦ Our Story

About AREWA SQUARE

We are not just a marketplace. We are a movement to dignify and digitise the Arewa trading spirit.

AREWA SQUARE was born from a simple truth: Northern Nigeria has millions of hardworking, honest traders who deserve a trusted digital home. For too long, buyers couldn't find reliable sellers online, and sellers struggled to reach customers beyond their street or market row.

We built AREWA SQUARE to change that. Every seller on our platform is manually verified by our team — we confirm real identities, real businesses, and real locations before a shop goes live. No fake profiles. No anonymous traders. Just real people with real businesses, accountable to their community.

Our permanent shop number system — inspired by the fixed stall numbers of Arewa's great markets like Kantin Kwari, Sabon Gari, and Farm Centre — gives every seller a lasting identity. Whether you sell shoes, food, phones, or services, your shop number follows your business and builds your reputation over time.
