🏪 Seller Guidelines

Seller Rules

Last updated: January 2026  |  All verified sellers must read and follow these rules.

🔢

Your Shop Number is a Trust Contract

When AREWA SQUARE assigns you a shop number like Shop #021, it is a permanent mark of trust. Buyers will know your number, remember your number, and return to your number. Guard it with honesty.

These Seller Rules exist to protect buyers and honest sellers alike. AREWA SQUARE is built on trust — and trust only works when every seller plays by the same rules. Violating these rules will result in permanent removal from the platform.

✅ What You CAN Do

  • List real products you genuinely sell
  • Upload your own product photos
  • Set your own fair prices
  • Communicate with buyers on WhatsApp or by phone
  • Update your product listings at any time
  • Offer delivery if you are able to
  • Ask buyers to visit your physical shop
  • Promote your shop number on social media
  • Report other sellers who break the rules

❌ What You CANNOT Do

  • Collect payment and fail to deliver goods
  • Use stock photos or images from other sellers
  • List fake, counterfeit, or stolen items
  • Provide a false address or phone number
  • Share your account login with another person
  • Create a second seller account under a different name
  • Harass, threaten, or abuse any buyer
  • Advertise products you do not have in stock
  • Inflate prices deceptively after a buyer contacts you

1. Verification Requirements

Before your shop goes live on AREWA SQUARE, you must:

1

Submit a valid Government ID

NIN slip, voter card, national ID card, or driver's licence
2

Upload a real photo of your shop or business

Must be a genuine photo of your stall, shop, or business location — not a logo or stock image
3

Provide an active phone number and WhatsApp

This number is what buyers will use to reach you — it must work
4

Wait for admin review (24–48 hours)

Our team will manually review and approve your application before assigning your shop number

2. Product Listing Standards

  • All product prices must be honest and consistent with what you tell buyers when they contact you.
  • Product photos must be of your actual items — not downloaded from the internet or copied from another brand.
  • If a product goes out of stock, you must remove or mark it unavailable within 48 hours.
  • Product descriptions must be accurate. Do not exaggerate quality, size, or features.
  • You are responsible for all content you upload to your shop.

3. Communication with Buyers

  • Respond to all buyer messages within 24 hours on business days.
  • Always be respectful and professional, even if a buyer is difficult.
  • Do not pressure buyers into purchases or send repeated unsolicited messages.
  • If a buyer requests a refund or has a complaint, make every effort to resolve it fairly.
  • Any disputes that cannot be resolved directly should be reported to AREWA SQUARE admin.

4. Zero Tolerance for Fraud

AREWA SQUARE has a zero-tolerance policy for any form of fraud. This includes:

  • Accepting payment from a buyer and not delivering the agreed product or service.
  • Providing false tracking information to appear as if an order was fulfilled.
  • Claiming a product was delivered when it was not.
  • Using a fake identity or someone else's ID to register a shop.

⚠️ Consequences of Fraud

  • Your shop number will be permanently revoked — it will never be reissued to you.
  • Your account will be immediately suspended and your data reported to the relevant authorities.
  • Your name and business will be added to the AREWA SQUARE blocked sellers list.
  • AREWA SQUARE will cooperate fully with any legal investigation.

5. Your Shop Number — Rights and Responsibilities

  • Your shop number (e.g. Shop #021) belongs to you and your registered business only.
  • You may not sell, lend, transfer, or share your shop number with any other person or business.
  • If you close your business and wish to transfer it to a family member, contact AREWA SQUARE admin for an official transfer review.
  • Impersonating another shop's number is grounds for immediate permanent removal.
  • Your shop number is displayed on your profile and in all order confirmations — use it proudly and protect it carefully.

6. Warnings, Suspension & Removal

1

First Warning

A written warning from AREWA SQUARE admin explaining the violation
2

Temporary Suspension

Your shop will be hidden from buyers for a defined period while the issue is resolved
3

Permanent Removal

For serious violations (fraud, repeated offences, abuse) — shop number revoked permanently, no appeal

Note: AREWA SQUARE reserves the right to skip directly to permanent removal for serious violations without a prior warning.
