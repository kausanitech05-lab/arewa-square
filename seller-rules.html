Seller Rules &
Guidelines

These rules protect buyers, protect honest sellers, and protect the reputation of AREWA SQUARE. Read them before you apply.

🗓 Last updated: June 2025  •  All sellers must comply
✅ What You Must Do 🚫 What is Not Allowed ⚖️ Consequences 🤝 Our Commitment to You
✅

What You Must Do

These are the basic standards every verified seller on AREWA SQUARE is required to maintain at all times.

1
🪪
Use Real and Accurate Business Information

Your shop name, phone number, WhatsApp number, business address, state, and LGA must all be genuine and up to date. Buyers rely on this information to find and contact you. If anything changes — a new phone number, a new address — update your shop profile within 7 days.

Required at all times
2
💬
Respond to Buyers Within a Reasonable Time

When a buyer contacts you by phone or WhatsApp, respond within a reasonable time. Buyers who are ignored will report the shop, which damages your standing on the platform. You are not required to be available 24/7, but if you are receiving contacts through AREWA SQUARE, buyers expect a response. Set your WhatsApp business hours if needed.

Expected standard
3
📦
Only List Products You Actually Have Available

Every product you add to your shop must be something you currently stock and can supply. Do not add products to appear more stocked than you are. If an item runs out, remove or mark it as unavailable promptly. Buyers who are told a product is unavailable after contacting you will leave negative feedback.

Honesty required
4
💰
Keep Your Prices Accurate and Transparent

Product prices on your shop page must reflect what you actually charge. You may not quote a lower price publicly to attract buyers and then increase the price when they contact you. Price changes are allowed but must be updated on your product listing first. Additional fees like delivery must be clearly communicated to the buyer upfront.

No hidden fees
5
🔄
Keep Your Shop Updated When Things Change

If your business relocates, temporarily closes, changes its phone number, or you stop selling a category of products, update your shop profile immediately. Buyers who visit a shop based on outdated information waste their time and will report the listing. An inactive or outdated shop may be suspended after two formal warnings.

Update promptly
6
🤝
Treat Every Buyer With Respect

Every buyer who contacts you through AREWA SQUARE is a potential customer. Treat them professionally and courteously regardless of whether they place an order. Rude, dismissive, or aggressive behaviour toward buyers will be actioned immediately on receipt of a credible report.

Basic standard
🚫

What is NOT Allowed

These are absolute prohibitions. Any of the following will result in immediate investigation and may lead to permanent removal.

1
🚫
No Fake, Counterfeit, or Misleading Listings

Do not list counterfeit goods as genuine. Do not use misleading photos, descriptions, or brand names to make products appear more valuable than they are. Do not list items you do not have in stock and do not intend to supply. Every product listing must accurately represent what the buyer will receive.

Zero tolerance
2
⚠️
No Illegal Goods or Services

Listing, promoting, or selling illegal items — including weapons, controlled substances, stolen goods, pirated software, or anything prohibited under Nigerian law — is an immediate and permanent ban. AREWA SQUARE cooperates fully with Nigerian law enforcement agencies when illegal activity is reported.

Permanent ban + legal action
3
💸
No Fraud or Collecting Payment Without Delivery

Accepting payment from a buyer and failing to deliver the agreed goods or services is fraud. Any seller who is credibly reported for collecting money without delivering will be permanently removed, their identity and shop number will be flagged in our records, and the case may be referred to the Nigerian Consumer Protection Council (FCCPC) and police.

Permanent ban + legal referral
4
👥
No Multiple Accounts

Each individual person or business may have only one verified shop on AREWA SQUARE. Creating a second account — whether to bypass a suspension, avoid a bad record, or list more businesses — is not permitted. Multiple accounts detected on the same device, phone number, or ID will result in all accounts being suspended pending review.

One account per seller
5
😡
No Harassment, Threats, or Abuse

Threatening, harassing, intimidating, or abusing any buyer — or any member of the AREWA SQUARE team — through any channel is grounds for immediate suspension. This includes aggressive messages on WhatsApp, threatening phone calls, or any behaviour that makes a buyer feel unsafe.

Immediate suspension
6
📍
No Businesses Outside the Covered States

AREWA SQUARE is exclusively for businesses physically operating within the 20 Northern Nigerian states and Abuja. Registering with a false address in a covered state when your business is elsewhere is a violation of our verification policy and will result in removal once discovered.

Eligibility requirement

⚖️ Consequences of Violations

First Offence (minor) — A formal written warning is sent to your registered email. Your shop remains live. You are given 48 hours to correct the issue. Failure to correct results in temporary suspension.

Temporary Suspension — Your shop is hidden from buyers while the issue is reviewed. Your shop number is reserved for you. If the issue is resolved satisfactorily, your shop is reinstated. If not, it becomes permanent.

Permanent Removal — Your shop is removed from AREWA SQUARE. Your shop number is retired and will never be re-assigned. Your verified identity remains in our records and you cannot re-register on the platform.

Legal Referral — For fraud, illegal goods, or serious criminal activity, AREWA SQUARE reserves the right to report the matter — along with your verified identity documents — to the Nigerian Police Force, FCCPC, or other relevant authorities.

Appeals: Sellers who believe a suspension or removal was in error may appeal by emailing support@arewasquare.com within 7 days of notification. Appeals are reviewed within 5 business days.

🔢
