✦ Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we receive most often about how verification and the platform works.

How long does seller approval take? ▼
Applications are reviewed within 24 to 48 hours of submission during business days (Monday to Saturday). If your application is submitted on a Friday evening, you may receive your response on Monday morning. You will be notified by email regardless of the outcome.
What happens if my application is rejected? ▼
You will receive an email explaining why your application was not approved. Common reasons include a blurry ID photo, a shop photo that does not clearly show a business location, or a business outside our covered states. You are welcome to correct the issue and re-apply immediately at no cost.
Is my government ID stored safely? ▼
Yes. Uploaded documents are stored on secure, encrypted servers (Cloudinary). They are only accessed by the AREWA SQUARE admin team for the purpose of verification and are never shared with third parties, buyers, or other sellers. Once verified, documents are not displayed publicly anywhere on the platform.
Can I change my shop number later? ▼
No. Shop numbers are permanent and non-transferable. This is by design — the permanence of a shop number means buyers can trust it, refer back to it, and know it belongs to one real business. If your business name changes, you can update it, but your number stays the same.
Which states are covered by AREWA SQUARE? ▼
AREWA SQUARE covers all 20 Northern Nigerian states plus Abuja (FCT): Abuja, Adamawa, Bauchi, Benue, Borno, Gombe, Jigawa, Kaduna, Kano, Katsina, Kebbi, Kogi, Kwara, Nasarawa, Niger, Plateau, Sokoto, Taraba, Yobe, and Zamfara. Businesses physically located outside these states are not eligible at this time.
Is registration free? Are there any fees? ▼
Buyer registration is completely free with no hidden costs. Seller registration is also free. An optional subscription plan for sellers unlocks additional features such as promoted listing and analytics. Basic shop listing and product uploads are always free.
I operate from home. Can I still register? ▼
Yes. Home-based businesses are welcome. For your shop photo, simply upload a clear photo of your workspace, product display, or home setup. As long as we can confirm you are operating a real business from a real location within the covered states, your application will be considered.
Does AREWA SQUARE handle payments between buyers and sellers? ▼
No. AREWA SQUARE is a discovery and connection platform. All payments and delivery arrangements are made directly between the buyer and the seller via WhatsApp or phone. We do not process, hold, or guarantee any payments. We encourage buyers to use trusted payment methods and to report any sellers who behave dishonestly.

Algorithms miss things. A real person reviewing your application means fewer fake shops, fewer scams, and a marketplace that buyers in Northern Nigeria can actually trust. That is what AREWA SQUARE is built on.
